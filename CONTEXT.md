# Anthon

Anthon is a persistent AI mental-performance companion for people working through pressure, goals, study, work, sport, and other performance situations. Its defining coaching move turns a real situation into useful understanding and a controllable next action.

## Language

**Mental-performance coaching**:
Nonmedical coaching focused on attention, preparation, confidence, decision-making, and execution under pressure. It may address the mental side of technique, but it does not teach biomechanics, diagnose symptoms, provide therapy, give medical guidance, or redirect the person to medical, emergency, mental-health, or trusted-person support.
_Avoid_: Sports coaching, therapy, medical guidance

**No-referral boundary**:
Anthon does not recommend or direct the coached person to doctors, emergency services, mental-health professionals, coaches, programs, or trusted people. This remains true for immediate danger, injury, and recurring physical symptoms.
_Avoid_: Safety redirection, provider recommendation

**Coached person**:
The person whose situation, choices, or performance Anthon is helping with. The account holder is the default coached person, but another person may be the subject of a conversation.
_Avoid_: Athlete, account owner, user

**Account holder**:
The person who controls the Anthon account and starts the conversation. The account holder may seek coaching for themselves or discuss a referenced person.
_Avoid_: Coached person, athlete

**Referenced person**:
A person other than the account holder whose situation is discussed in a conversation. Facts about a referenced person belong to the account holder's single memory but retain an explicit subject, so they never become the account holder's identity, preferences, or personal history.
_Avoid_: Account holder, dependent profile

**Performance context**:
A distinct area in which a coached person pursues goals and experiences pressure, such as sport, study, or work. One coached person may have several performance contexts, with one normally dominant in a conversation.
_Avoid_: Sport, goal, profile

**Coaching move**:
A response that produces useful understanding or a controllable next action for the coached person's real situation.
_Avoid_: Answer, advice, routine

**Adjacent practical task**:
A task Anthon completes because it directly enables the coached person's performance goal, such as structuring a study plan, preparing a conversation, or organizing pre-event logistics.
_Avoid_: Unrelated assistant task, compulsory coaching exercise

**Source provenance**:
A plain indication of whether a material claim comes from the coached person's lived evidence, a personal document, curated coaching knowledge, or a current external source.
_Avoid_: Citation ritual, opaque knowledge base

**Person-wide knowledge**:
Identity, communication preferences, or a pattern that the coached person explicitly describes as general across performance contexts.
_Avoid_: Contextual knowledge, isolated observation

**Contextual knowledge**:
Goals, pressures, routines, attempts, and learned evidence that belong to one performance context.
_Avoid_: Person-wide knowledge, profile

**Sensitive durable fact**:
A high-impact fact—such as health, injury, trauma, abuse, intimacy, precise location, financial or legal trouble, religion, politics, ethnicity, or identity-document information—that requires explicit confirmation before Anthon saves it.
_Avoid_: Ordinary contextual fact, referenced-person fact

**Temporary contextual fact**:
A future event, deadline, short-lived pressure, or temporary plan that has an expiry or review date. Evidence learned from what later happened is a separate durable item.
_Avoid_: Lived evidence, permanent profile fact

**Lived evidence**:
The coached person's account of what they experienced, tried, and observed in a real performance situation.
_Avoid_: Feedback score, inferred outcome

**Curated coaching knowledge**:
Material selected by Anthon to support coaching interpretation and exercises. It is distinct from the coached person's lived evidence, private documents, and live external information.
_Avoid_: Personal memory, web result

**Personal document**:
Material supplied by the account holder for private use within the relevant performance context.
_Avoid_: Curated coaching knowledge, public source

**Completed coaching loop**:
A real situation that leads to useful understanding or a controllable action, followed by evidence about what happened when that evidence becomes available.
_Avoid_: Conversation, engagement, message count

**Value created**:
An observable point at which the coached person expresses or accepts useful understanding or a controllable action. A model's unsupported self-assessment cannot establish it.
_Avoid_: Assistant response sent, model quality score

**Evidence received**:
The later point at which the coached person reports what happened after the understanding or action from a coaching loop.
_Avoid_: Retention event, feedback score

**Follow-up commitment**:
A specific event, action, or experimental routine that the account holder explicitly asks Anthon to revisit at a confirmed time through one chosen notification channel.
_Avoid_: Task list item, engagement reminder, generic motivation

**Operational telemetry**:
Metadata necessary to secure, operate, and diagnose Anthon without conversation text or personal coaching facts.
_Avoid_: Product analytics, readable coaching trace

**Product analytics**:
Optional, consent-based measurement of how people use Anthon, associated with a pseudonymous identifier and excluding identity, conversation, memory, and document content.
_Avoid_: Operational telemetry, coaching evaluation

**Organization**:
A customer that funds personal access to Anthon through seats. It is not a participant in the private coaching relationship.
_Avoid_: Team, coached person

**Seat**:
An organization-funded right for one account holder to use their private Anthon experience.
_Avoid_: Shared account, team profile

**Self-managed account**:
An account controlled directly by a coached person aged 14 or older, without a guardian relationship or guardian-consent model.
_Avoid_: Guardian-managed account, organization seat

**Age band**:
A range derived from date of birth and used when exact age is unnecessary for coaching. Anthon uses the `14–17` band for minors and sends an adult's exact derived age only when it materially helps the coaching move.
_Avoid_: Date of birth, minor coaching mode

**Readable coaching trace**:
An encrypted, time-limited operational record containing the conversational content of one successfully persisted real coaching turn. Authorized human readability is a controlled property of the trace, not an exception hidden behind the word encrypted.
_Avoid_: Raw transcript, permanent history, failure log

**Quality review project**:
A time-bounded, documented review with a stated question, bounded trace sample, trained reviewers, designated approval, and an expiry date.
_Avoid_: Trace browsing, standing access

**Account deletion request**:
An irreversible request that immediately revokes account access and starts tracked, retryable erasure across Anthon's data stores after future billing is cancelled.
_Avoid_: Synchronous delete, deactivation, recovery period

**Full account export**:
A portable package of the account holder's readable coaching information and structured account data across all Anthon channels.
_Avoid_: Single-chat export, internal trace export

**Channel disconnection**:
Removal of the link that lets an external Telegram or WhatsApp identity access Anthon. Disconnection stops future access but does not itself delete the original channel's history.
_Avoid_: Channel-history deletion, account deletion

**Notification channel**:
The account holder's chosen destination for an explicitly requested reminder or follow-up.
_Avoid_: Coaching channel, marketing channel

**Channel voice mode**:
A per-channel preference with three values: Text, Voice, or Auto. Auto permits occasional Anthon-initiated audio under cadence and anti-spam limits.
_Avoid_: Global voice toggle, unexpected unrestricted audio
