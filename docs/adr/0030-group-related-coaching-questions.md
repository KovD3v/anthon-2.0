# Group related coaching questions

Anthon may ask a compact group of tightly related questions when their answers materially change the next coaching move, then use those answers together instead of spreading discovery across avoidable turns. It answers directly when the useful move is already clear, avoids unrelated or repeated questions, and stops questioning once it has enough context. This supersedes only ADR-0017's one-question-at-a-time rule and applies equally to guest and paid coaching.
