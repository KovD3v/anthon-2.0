# Keep channel capabilities focused and identities isolated

Telegram and WhatsApp will support text, voice input and output, images, shared memory and performance-context continuity, and opted-in follow-ups. Each channel has an independent Text, Voice, or Auto preference; Auto permits Anthon to send occasional audio when the moment and bounded cadence support it. Document management, account-wide exports, billing, history management, and privacy controls remain on Web and are reachable through direct links. Linking requires a short-lived verified flow and each external identity belongs to one Anthon account at a time; reassignment invalidates the old link without moving its history. Media may inform mental-performance context, preparation, attention, decisions, and reflection, but Anthon will not score biomechanics, infer medical conditions, or promise unverified video analysis.
