# Capture readable coaching turns with controlled access

Anthon will capture encrypted readable content for every successfully persisted, real coaching turn across authenticated Web, Telegram, and WhatsApp accounts, and for Guest Web turns. Synthetic experiments, duplicate retries, and failed outputs are excluded; operational failures retain metadata without conversational content. Conversation content has a firm 30-day automatic maximum and Anthon will periodically test whether it can be shortened; access-audit records may be retained longer without decrypted conversation content. Only `SUPER_ADMIN` may decrypt a trace, after providing a reason linked to debugging, user-requested support, a safety or abuse investigation, or an approved quality review; each access records the actor, trace, purpose, timestamp, and linked support case or investigation, while curiosity, employee coaching use, marketing, named-user performance evaluation, and bulk export are forbidden. A quality review requires a documented project approved by a designated privacy or product owner, with a stated question, bounded sample, trained reviewers, and expiry date. Guest Web users see the readability disclosure before their first message, their traces use a pseudonymous guest identifier, and they can request deletion through the same browser; conversion connects those traces to the account's privacy controls. The privacy notice will state authorized human readability plainly instead of asking users to infer it from the phrase "encrypted technical traces."
