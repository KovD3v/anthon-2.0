# Make memory visible, revisable, and time-aware

Anthon may save an ordinary contextual fact without interrupting coaching, while showing a small non-conversational memory-updated indicator with undo and keeping every durable item inspectable in the profile. Sensitive facts—including health, injury, trauma, abuse, intimacy, precise location, financial or legal trouble, religion, politics, ethnicity, identity documents, and comparable high-impact information—require explicit confirmation; referenced-person facts are never saved. Future events, deadlines, temporary pressures, and short-lived plans receive an expiry or review date, while evidence about what later happened may remain contextually durable. A newest explicit correction becomes active immediately; the replaced value remains only in inspectable revision history and is excluded from coaching context unless restored.
