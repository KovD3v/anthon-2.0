# Keep coaching data owned by the account holder

Removing an organization-funded seat ends only that entitlement; the account holder keeps their account, conversations, memories, performance contexts, and Web access to data and privacy controls. Coaching continues only when another personal or organization-funded paid entitlement is active. Disconnecting Telegram or WhatsApp stops future channel access but preserves history in its original thread, with a separate explicit option to disconnect and delete that channel's Anthon history. Deleting an individual conversation shows its linked durable items and deletes contextual facts and evidence by default, retaining person-wide knowledge only when the account holder explicitly chooses to keep it. These lifecycle rules prevent a payer, organization, channel provider, or deleted source conversation from silently taking ownership of personal coaching data.
