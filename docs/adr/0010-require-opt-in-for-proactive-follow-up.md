# Require opt-in for proactive follow-up

Anthon may create a follow-up commitment only after the account holder opts in for a specific event, action, or experimental routine and confirms its subject, delivery time, and one notification channel. Launch channels are Web push and connected Telegram or WhatsApp; email is deferred. The Web profile owns the default channel, timezone, quiet hours, and preview-detail setting. An explicitly confirmed delivery time may override quiet hours after the interface makes that clear. Notifications use minimal lock-screen content by default, never duplicate or silently fall back across channels, and are not delivered late after failure. A missed commitment remains visible on Web and may be revisited once on a relevant natural return until answered, closed, or expired. Anthon will not send generic motivational, engagement, repeated, or guilt-inducing messages.
